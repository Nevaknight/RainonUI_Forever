## 0.1.1

- Redesigned the settings window to match the Forever spellbook, with framed icons, top tabs, and a minimap button toggle.
- Added a Characters tab with rested XP bars sorted from highest to lowest, offline estimates, per-character tracking and Well Rested settings, and controls to remove or restore characters.
- Moved the reload button inside the Esc menu, where it runs the built-in `/reload` command.
- Updated the seal macro and crate tooltip text to avoid separators that could display as a square.
