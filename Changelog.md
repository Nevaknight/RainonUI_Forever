# RainonUI Forever — Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Added Paladin loot rankings to item tooltips, a current-dungeon loot window, and a filterable dungeon loot catalog. Right-click the minimap icon for the current dungeon or Shift-click it to open the catalog. Dungeon windows use ForeverDungeonJournal for loot data.
- Added optional group loot tools to show who rolled Need and automatically roll Greed for selected item qualities, with configurable exceptions.
- Added item notes, an optional catalog of notes from Rainon, and Baganator integration for item notes and junk labels.
- Expanded the Paladin macro builder with mouseover macros and configurable Seal pairs.
- Updated the Characters tab to show the estimated full-rest date and hide characters at the level cap. You can pause tracking or remove a character from the list.
- Added a one-time update notice for existing installations, with a toggle in General settings.
- Removed the old supply-crate price tooltip.
